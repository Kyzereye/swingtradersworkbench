# Technical analysis systems 1

Well-known stock TA systems / frameworks. Order is recognition, not proven
returns. One example of the type is moving-average crossover.

1. **Moving average crossover** — fast MA crosses slow MA (or price vs MA) for trend entries/exits
2. **Trend following / breakout** — buy new highs or range breakouts, trail or exit on opposite break (Donchian-style)
3. **Mean reversion / RSI** — fade overbought/oversold back to a mean
4. **MACD system** — signal-line cross, zero-line cross, histogram turns
5. **Bollinger Band system** — walk the bands in trends, or fade touches / squeeze breakouts
6. **Ichimoku** — cloud, tenkan/kijun cross, price vs cloud as a full ruleset
7. **Support / resistance & swing structure** — higher highs/lows, breaks and retests of levels
8. **Fibonacci retracement / extension** — pullback entries and target extensions in a trend
9. **Volume Profile / VWAP** — trade relative to value area, POC, or VWAP
10. **Candlestick pattern systems** — engulfing, pin bars, etc., usually with trend filter
11. **Elliott Wave** — impulse/corrective wave counts for direction and targets
12. **Wyckoff** — accumulation/distribution, springs, signs of strength/weakness
13. **Point & figure** — box/reversal charting, column breaks and count targets
14. **Seasonality / calendar systems** — month, turn-of-month, earnings/window patterns (more timing than pure chart TA)
15. **Multi-timeframe trend alignment** — higher TF trend filter + lower TF entry (e.g. weekly trend, daily trigger)

# Technical analysis systems 2

While individual indicators (like RSI or standard moving averages) provide raw data signals, **technical analysis systems** combine entries, exits, risk rules, and execution conditions into repeatable trading frameworks.

### Trend-Following Systems

1. **Donchian Breakout System (Turtle Trading System)**
* **Core Logic:** Buys when price breaks above the highest high of $N$ days (traditionally 20 or 55 days) and sells when price drops below the lowest low of $N$ days.
* **How It Works:** Built on absolute price channels, this system ignores indicators and relies purely on multi-week range expansions to catch major extended trends while cutting losses fast.

2. **Ichimoku Kinko Hyo System**
* **Core Logic:** Uses a 5-line overlay (Tenkan, Kijun, Senkou Spans A/B, Chikou) to form a dynamic "Cloud" representing multi-period dynamic support/resistance.
* **How It Works:** Trades are triggered when price exits the cloud, confirmed by a Tenkan/Kijun line cross above/below the cloud boundaries.

3. **Triple Moving Average Alignment System**
* **Core Logic:** Utilizes three distinct timeframes (e.g., 10-day, 20-day, and 50-day EMAs) to establish market regime.
* **How It Works:** Enters long only when the fastest EMA is above the medium, and the medium is above the slowest, requiring full multi-frame stacking before taking a trend trade.

4. **Keltner Channel Trend System**
* **Core Logic:** Encloses price within an Exponential Moving Average envelope scaled by Average True Range (ATR).
* **How It Works:** System enters long when price closes cleanly *above* the upper band (indicating a breakout with high volatility), using the center EMA or opposite band as a trailing stop.

### Mean-Reversion & Volatility Systems

5. **Bollinger Band Squeeze & Reversion System**
* **Core Logic:** Pairs Bollinger Bands with a volatility contraction metric (like Keltner Channels or BandWidth).
* **How It Works:** System identifies tight consolidations ("squeezes"), trades the sudden outer-band breakout, and exits when price reverts back to the 20-period middle SMA.

6. **RSI Failure Swing System**
* **Core Logic:** Uses momentum divergence rather than simple overbought/oversold levels to capture tops and bottoms.
* **How It Works:** If RSI enters overbought territory (>70), drops back below 70, bounces, fails to make a higher high, and then breaks its previous swing low, the system executes a short entry.

7. **VWAP Reversion System**
* **Core Logic:** Anchors to institutional Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) as an intraday fair-value benchmark.
* **How It Works:** Trades entries when price stretches 2+ standard deviations away from the intraday VWAP, targeting a full mean-reversion move back to the central VWAP line.

### Price Action & Structural Systems

8. **Wyckoff Accumulation / Distribution System**
* **Core Logic:** Evaluates price cycles across 4 phases: Accumulation, Markup, Distribution, and Markdown.
* **How It Works:** Maps institutional supply and demand by trading the "Spring" (false breakdown below support) or the "Sign of Strength" (confirmed breakout above the trading range).

9. **TheStrat (#TheStrat) Multi-Timeframe System**
* **Core Logic:** Categorizes candles into 3 types (1 = Inside, 2 = Directional, 3 = Outside) across multiple timeframes.
* **How It Works:** Executes trades based on multi-timeframe continuity setups (e.g., a "2-1-2 continuation" or "2-2 reversal" triggering across the daily and weekly charts simultaneously).

10. **Market Structure / Smart Money Concepts (SMC) System**
* **Core Logic:** Focuses on Liquidity Sweeps, Change of Character (ChoCH), and Fair Value Gaps (FVG).
* **How It Works:** Enters trades when price takes out key swing highs/lows (liquidity sweep), shifts market structure, and pullbacks to fill an unmitigated order block or FVG.

11. **Japanese Candlestick Reversal System**
* **Core Logic:** Uses multi-bar candlestick structural patterns (Engulfing, Morning/Evening Stars, Three White Soldiers) at defined key technical boundaries.
* **How It Works:** Requires a 2-or-3-candle pattern to complete *at* major macro support or resistance, taking entries in the direction of the pattern's structural reversal.

### Range & Breakout Systems

12. **Opening Range Breakout (ORB) System**
* **Core Logic:** Establishes the high and low price levels established during the first market window (e.g., first 15 or 30 minutes of the trading day).
* **How It Works:** Triggers long or short positions as soon as price breaks and closes outside the opening boundary on above-average relative volume.

13. **Volume Profile Point of Control (POC) System**
* **Core Logic:** Uses horizontal volume distribution rather than vertical daily volume to identify high-volume nodes (HVN) and low-volume nodes (LVN).
* **How It Works:** Treats the Point of Control (POC—price with the most traded volume) as a target/magnet and trades quick moves through LVNs (areas of low liquidity).

14. **Gartley / Harmonic Pattern System**
* **Core Logic:** Uses strict Fibonacci ratios to map specific geometric price structures (Gartley, Bat, Butterfly, Crab).
* **How It Works:** System identifies Potential Reversal Zones (PRZ) where specific leg ratios align (e.g., a 0.618 retracement of leg X-A paired with a 1.272 extension of B-C), entering as the pattern completes.

15. **Darvas Box System**
* **Core Logic:** A momentum-breakout system invented by Nicolas Darvas that isolates stocks reaching new 52-week highs.
* **How It Works:** Traps price movement inside a "box" defined by the new high and a subsequent floor; enters long when price breaks above the top of the upper box line, carrying a tight trailing stop at the box bottom.
